# 06 — Organize visible Community channels

**What to build:** Authorized Members can organize the Community into ordered Categories, Text Channels, and Voice Rooms while Role and channel Permission rules completely hide spaces a Member may not discover.

**Blocked by:** 04 — Manage Roles and ownership; 05 — Invite and manage Member profiles

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Authorized Members create, edit, reorder, archive, and restore Categories, Text Channels, and Voice Rooms.
- [x] Community-wide Role Permissions combine with channel-specific allow and deny overrides deterministically.
- [x] Members without Channel Visibility cannot list, fetch, infer, or subscribe to the hidden space.
- [x] Gaining visibility grants access to retained Text Channel history rather than only future content.
- [x] Archived spaces retain their identity and history while leaving normal active navigation.
- [x] Permanent deletion is Owner-only, separately confirmed, and rejects accidental or stale confirmations.
- [x] The embedded UI and versioned API expose identical authorization behavior.
