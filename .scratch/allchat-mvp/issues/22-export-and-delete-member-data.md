# 22 — Export and delete Member data

**What to build:** A Member can obtain a portable account export and irreversibly anonymize their identity while preserving shared conversation continuity and the other Direct Message participant's retained history.

**Blocked by:** 11 — Upload and safely retrieve Attachments; 13 — Exchange Direct Messages and enforce Blocks; 14 — Report and moderate Community activity; 21 — Back up, restore, and migrate an Instance

**Status:** resolved

- [x] A Member export includes their profile, authored Messages, Direct Message history, and authorized Attachment references in a documented portable form.
- [x] Export excludes hidden Community content and unrelated Members' private/profile data.
- [x] Members can delete individual authored Messages before Account Deletion.
- [x] Account Deletion requires fresh authentication and explicit irreversible confirmation.
- [x] Deletion anonymizes identity and revokes Sessions while preserving otherwise retained Community and DM conversation content.
- [x] Moderation Records retain necessary action accountability without retaining deleted Message bodies or active profile identity unnecessarily.
- [x] Backup/restore and search behavior remain consistent after anonymization.
